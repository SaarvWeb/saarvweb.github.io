ABOUT US

Why do cross-border growth opportunities so often get “lost in translation”?
Not merely in language, but in regulations, business practices, market expectations and cultural understanding.

SAARV-A Consultancy exists to bridge these gaps. Based in Finland, we work at the intersection of Europe and India, bringing practical understanding of the legal, regulatory, corporate and institutional environments in both — turning cross-border opportunities into workable collaborations.

We work with two audiences: SMEs expanding across borders, and research institutions looking to put their expertise to work in industry.. Across sectors ranging from technology and tourism to industry and research, we find the right partners, navigate unfamiliar environments, and move ambitions towards execution.

Our name. SAARV-A draws on the Sanskrit word Sarva — "whole or complete". It reflects how we work: looking beyond individual transactions to the bigger picture of people, relationships, institutions and opportunities that make a partnership succeed. The "-A" is you — our client. The whole is never complete without you; your ambitions give our work its direction. The golden arc in our identity is a bridge: connecting markets, perspectives, and opportunities — the distance between where you are and where you aim to be.

Sun or snow. Here or there. We build the bridge.

How we help:
Know Before You Go: We map out the rules, competitiors, and cost risks in your target market — so you know what your are walking into before your spend. We look at the practical realities of entering the market and where your offer fits.
